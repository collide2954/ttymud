# Help, man, ?, and suggestions for typos.
The Relay
A crowded public node.
Routes: market, back-room, bazaar
Files: motd.txt, rules.txt
> ?
Here you can use: look, ls, go, read, download, inventory, status, stash, grep, say, channels, join, leave, who, news, crew, tell, ignore, block, report, help
> help go
go <place>
Move to a host through a route.
Other names: cd, connect, ssh
Example: go market
> man pipes
Pipelines
Join commands with | to send the items of one command to the next.
Only the last command shows its output.
Programs in one pipeline share one setup cycle and one connection.
Example: ls | grep txt | read
> help | grep rig
read <file>       Show the text of a file here or on your rig.
download <file>   Copy a file from this host to your rig.
inventory         Show the files on your rig.
buy <item>        Buy a program or a rig upgrade at a shop.
