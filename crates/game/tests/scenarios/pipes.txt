# Pipelines pass items from one command to the next.
The Relay
A crowded public node.
Routes: market, back-room, bazaar
Files: motd.txt, rules.txt
> ls | grep a | grep ba
back-room/
bazaar/
> read motd.txt | grep polite
Be polite.
> ls | grep baz | go
The Bazaar
Everything is for sale.
Routes: relay, market
> look | ls
ls takes no input from a pipe.
> grep x
grep needs input from a pipe. Example: ls | grep txt
> back | ls
back gives no output to a pipe.
> ls |
Each side of | needs a command.
